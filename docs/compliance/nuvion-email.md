# Draft: email to Nuvion

**Subject:** Kova on Nuvion: licence coverage and payouts on behalf of our users

Hi Nuvion team,

We run Kova (mykova.app), a self-custody dollar wallet on Monad. We use Nuvion for two things: cash-outs
to bank accounts in 35+ countries, and virtual dollar cards. Before we grow, we want to be sure our
setup sits properly on your licences.

How it works today:
- Each person who cashes out abroad or gets a card is onboarded as an individual child entity under
  our platform entity, with your ID check (ID document, proof of address, BVN/NIN or SSN).
- For a cash-out, we book-transfer the amount from our platform account into the person's own USD
  account, then send the payout from their account with their `entity_id`, in their name.

Our questions:
1. Which Nuvion entity and licence covers these payouts, and in which countries? Could you send us the
   licence or registration details for our files?
2. Is paying out on behalf of each verified child entity, as above, the setup you expect from a
   platform like ours? Is there anything we should change?
3. What do book transfers between our account and our users' accounts cost?
4. Can a child entity hold a USC (stablecoin) account with a wallet address on Base, receive USDC
   there, and have it converted to USD and paid out from their own account? We'd like our users'
   dollars to go straight into their own Nuvion account, so we never hold or exchange them.
5. Do you screen sanctions and limits on your side for each child entity's payouts, and are there
   per-person limits we should show in our app?
6. In your view, does this model need any licence of our own in Nigeria, where most of our users are?
