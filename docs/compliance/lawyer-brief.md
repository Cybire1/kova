# Brief for a Nigerian fintech lawyer

One page on what Kova is and does, and the questions we need answered.

## What Kova is

Kova (mykova.app, an iOS app and an Android app) is a self-custody dollar wallet and shop till, made
by Quill Labs. Balances are AUSD, a dollar stablecoin issued by Agora, on the Monad blockchain. Each
wallet's keys come from the person's passkey (Face ID or fingerprint) or their Google sign-in. Kova
can't move a person's money without their signature, and pays the network fee for them.

## What it does, and who does it

- **Send, receive, pay, shops:** person to person, on Monad. Kova relays signed payments; it never holds them.
- **Naira in and out:** through PAJ, a licensed partner. Kova's contract swaps AUSD to USDC and sends it
  straight to PAJ, which pays the naira. Kova doesn't hold the money in between.
- **Cash-out to banks in 35+ countries:** through Nuvion. Each person passes Nuvion's ID check, and
  the payout goes from their own Nuvion account, in their name. Kova's Nuvion account funds it for
  each payout, in exchange for the person's AUSD.
- **Dollar card:** Nuvion virtual cards, on the person's own verified Nuvion account.
- **Spending keys:** cards, allowances, AI agents, teammates and websites (Kova for Chrome) can spend
  from a wallet within limits set on Monad.
- **Tokenized stocks (xStocks by Backed):** buying paused; people can still sell what they hold.
- **Basic phones:** a USSD menu.

## Questions

1. Does Kova need to register with the SEC (for example as a VASP or under the ARIP incubation
   programme), given it's self-custody and routes exchanges to licensed partners? Does the 2026 draft
   digital asset rules change that?
2. Does the cash-out abroad model (Kova's Nuvion account funding the person's own account) make Kova
   an exchange or a money transmitter under SEC or CBN rules? Would sending the person's stablecoins
   straight to their own Nuvion account, so Kova never exchanges them, change the answer?
3. Can Kova offer tokenized foreign stocks to Nigerians at all, and if so, how?
4. What AML, KYC, record-keeping and reporting obligations fall on Kova itself?
5. What do we need under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPC registration, data audits)?
6. Anything in how we market Kova that we should change?
